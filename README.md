# Magic Poser (Android)

Native Kotlin & Jetpack Compose rewrite of Magic Poser Web using OpenGL ES 3.0.

## Features
- **3D Articulated Character Models**: 6 character proportions (`1:6 Anime Girl`, `1:6 Anime Boy`, `Woman`, `Man`, `1:7 Woman`, `1:5 Girl`) with hierarchical skeletal joints and 5-finger articulated hands.
- **10 Geometric Props**: Sphere, Cube, Cone, Cylinder, Hex, Pyramid, Rectangle, Stairs, Torus, and Wall with per-axis scaling and translation.
- **Interactive 3D Gizmos & Camera**: Tap raycasting to select joints, heads, characters, props, or the directional light; 3D XYZ rotation rings and translation arrows; orbit, pinch-to-zoom, and two-finger pan.
- **25 Body Pose Presets & 30 Hand Pose Presets**: Left/right hand toggle and instant preset application.
- **16 Hairstyles + Hairless Mode**: Attach and tint 3D hair meshes on character heads.
- **Material & Lighting Studio**: HSV color sliders, material swatches, 15 skin tone swatches, Body/Hair toggles, directional light brightness, and hemisphere GI strength.
- **Scene Controls & Localization**: Full Undo/Redo history, Duplicate, Replace Model, Lock, Preview Mode, Camera Presets (Center, Perspective FOV, Left/Right/Top/Bottom/Front/Back), Ground Grid, Sky Dome, Sportswear toggle, and English / 中文 / 日本語 UI switching.
